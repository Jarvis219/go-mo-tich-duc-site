How merit works

01

Tap the fish

Every knock is +1 merit. Sometimes +5 for a mindful tap, rarely +100 when the Buddha happens to be watching.

02

Shake or vent = karma

Shake the phone or press Vent: −1 merit, +1 karma. Ten taps in ten seconds to repent.

03

Spend it at the temple shop

Merit is the only currency: a gold fish, an RGB gaming fish, a payment-received ding. Your rank never drops.

Pointless, but with a ledger

Quick answers

Does it cost anything?

No. Completely free, no in-app purchases, no ads. Everything in the shop is bought with merit you tap out.

Is merit worth anything?

Only emotionally, and for bragging. It's a joke app, not spiritual advice.

Will I lose merit on a new phone?

Export a backup from Settings, or turn on backup to your own iCloud. There is no server holding it for you.

Does it make fun of Buddhism?

No. The jokes are about office life, deadlines and ourselves. The Buddha only watches and nods.
